In mine entry,Tracking point-1,tracking point-2,mine Exit the hardware are : 1-traffic light,sensor,RFID reader,MQTT based display,annoucement system,handheld rfid reader,1-boom barrier,I/O controller.
In Tare weight bridge,vadghat point,finall gross weight bridge point the hardware are : multiple traffic light,entry sensor,weightbridge sensor,exit sensor,RFID reader,MQTT based display,annoucement system,handheld rfid reader,multiple boom barrier,I/O controller,digitizer.in weight bridge case it can be unidirectional or bidirectional.
if unidirectional then two traffic light and two boom barrier,one for entry and one for exit.
if bidirectional then four traffic light,two boom barrier,two TL and one BB for entry and two TL and one BB for exit.
